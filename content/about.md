## What this is
A mitochondrial health guide built around three things: the [Mitochondrial Health Ratings](/mitohealth/), an evidence-graded database of foods, drugs, supplements and lifestyle habits; plain-language explainers on **VDAC1**, the mitochondrial channel implicated in a wide range of disease; and [research news](/news/), recent papers summarized and linked to their source.

## How ratings are graded
Each entry in the ratings database gets three scores: **evidence** (the quality of human data behind it, from preliminary to strong), **benefit** (the likely upside for mitochondrial function), and **safety**. Ratings lean on randomized trials and meta-analyses where they exist, and are labeled "preliminary" when they don't. Every entry links to its supporting research. The full methodology and rating scale are documented on the [ratings page](/mitohealth/).

This is a synthesis for orientation, not medical advice. Talk to a clinician before changing anything based on what's here.

::: callout
**Why mitochondria, why VDAC1?** Mitochondria don't just make energy: they hold the switches for inflammation and cell death too. VDAC1 sits at the crossroads, metabolites pass through it, calcium passes through it, and when a cell is in trouble it's part of how mitochondria decide to shut down. That crossroads shows up across a wide range of disease, which is why it gets particular attention here.
:::
