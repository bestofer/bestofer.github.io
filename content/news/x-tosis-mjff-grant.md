On **June 23, 2026**, **x-tosis** announced a **$2.74 million grant from The Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson's Research**, awarded through its Parkinson's Disease Therapeutics Pipeline Program. The funding advances **XTS001**, the company's lead candidate, toward clinical development.

XTS001 is a **brain-penetrant, orally available small molecule designed to inhibit VDAC1 oligomerization**, the process by which VDAC1 clumps into a large pore that leaks mtDNA, triggers inflammation, and contributes to neuron death. It comes off the company's **MitoXTS™ platform** of patented VDAC1-oligomerization inhibitors, building on **50+ years of work by Professor Varda Shoshan-Barmatz** in the same lineage as the [VBIT compounds](/posts/vbit-4-and-vbit-12/). In preclinical Parkinson's models, x-tosis reports XTS001 **reduced dopaminergic neuron loss and helped restore dopamine levels**.

It is a concrete test of the [mechanism](/posts/what-is-vdac1/) this site covers, aimed at a disease where energy-starved neurons die, now with a foundation grant behind it.
