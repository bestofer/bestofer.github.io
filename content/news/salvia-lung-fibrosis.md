Wei Zhang's team at Shuguang Hospital (Shanghai) showed danshen, *Salvia miltiorrhiza*, a staple of Chinese medicine, eases lung fibrosis in rats by nudging fibroblasts to run mitophagy through the PINK1/Parkin pathway. Nice to see a traditional remedy pinned to an actual quality-control switch rather than hand-waving.
