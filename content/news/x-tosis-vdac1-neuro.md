Time for the most conflicted post on the site, flagged as such.

**x-tosis** — the company I co-founded — just updated how it's talking about its work publicly, and since it lands squarely on this blog's obsession, I'm noting it here. The short version from [x-tosis.com](https://x-tosis.com): they're building **first-in-class VDAC1-modulating mitochondrial therapeutics** for neurodegeneration — **Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and ALS** — aiming *upstream*, at the biology of neuronal death itself, rather than at downstream symptoms. The tagline is "saving brain cells." They're based in Gainesville, Florida, and pairing the compounds with precision biomarkers for choosing and monitoring patients.

If you've read the rest of this site, you'll see why I can't be objective about this one.
